Provincial Nominee Program guidance

The Provincial Nominee Program allows Canadian provinces and territories to select individuals who have the skills, education, and work experience needed to contribute to their local economy. Each province operates its own immigration streams and selects applicants who intend to settle and build their future in that region.

How the PNP Works

1. Determine Eligibility

Each province has its own criteria, targeting skilled workers, international graduates, business owners, and individuals with specific work experience or job offers.

2. Apply to the Province

Applicants either submit an Expression of Interest (EOI) or apply directly to a provincial stream.

3. Receive Nomination

If selected, the province issues a nomination certificate confirming that the applicant meets its labour market and settlement needs.

4. Apply for Permanent Residence

After receiving the nomination, the applicant applies for permanent residence through the federal system. This may be through an Express Entry-aligned stream or a non-Express Entry stream.

Benefits of the PNP

  • A provincial nomination significantly increases the chance of permanent residence approval, especially for Express Entry applicants.
  • Many streams allow applicants to include spouses, partners, and dependent children.
  • PNP pathways often support applicants who may not meet the high federal CRS score requirements.
  • It offers a clear route to settle in a province where your occupation or skills are in demand.
