Mischief defence guidance

A mischief charge places freedom, record, and future at risk. Police treat property related offences seriously. Courts impose lasting consequences after conviction. Early defence action protects rights.

BG Laws defends clients charged with mischief across Ontario.

What Counts as Mischief

Mischief involves intentional interference with property or data belonging to another person. Common allegations include:

  • Damaging or destroying property.
  • Altering property without permission.
  • Making property unsafe or unusable.
  • Blocking lawful use or enjoyment of property.
  • Interfering with computer data or systems.

Accidental damage does not meet the legal standard. The Crown must prove intent.

Types of Mischief Charges

Charges vary based on value and circumstances:

  • Mischief under $5,000.
  • Mischief over $5,000.
  • Mischief involving data or infrastructure.
  • Mischief causing danger to life.

Charge type affects potential penalties and court procedure.

Penalties Upon Conviction

Mischief carries serious consequences:

  • Jail exposure up to two years less a day for summary matters.
  • Jail exposure up to ten years for indictable matters.
  • Life imprisonment exposure where conduct risks life.
  • Criminal record affecting work, travel, housing, and status.

Courts assess value, intent, and harm.

Defence Strategy at BG Laws

Effective defence focuses on facts and legal thresholds. Common defence issues include:

  • Lack of intent or accidental conduct.
  • Ownership or right to use the property.
  • Exaggerated damage claims or value disputes.
  • Identification errors.
  • Charter breaches during investigation or arrest.

The Crown must prove every element beyond reasonable doubt.
