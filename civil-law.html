Civil Law

Laws governing property rights, contracts, family relations, and inheritance

Civil Code

Adopted: 1951 Last Updated: 2023

The foundational civil law that covers property rights, contracts, family law, inheritance, and obligations.

Overview

The Civil Code is the principal legal instrument governing private law relationships between individuals and legal entities. It establishes the rights and responsibilities of parties in civil matters, including the formation and enforcement of contracts, property ownership and transfers, and family relationships.

Major Sections
  • General Provisions: Legal persons, capacity, representation
  • Property Rights: Ownership, possession, use, and disposal of property
  • Obligations: Formation, performance, and breach of contracts
  • Specific Contracts: Sale, lease, services, loan, and other agreements
  • Family Law: Marriage, divorce, parental rights, guardianship
  • Succession: Inheritance, wills, and succession procedures
Modern Amendments

The Civil Code has been updated to address e-commerce, consumer protection, property disputes, and contemporary social issues. Recent amendments strengthen protections for vulnerable persons and improve procedures for contract disputes.

Law on Family Relations

Adopted: 1985 Last Updated: 2024

Regulates marriage, divorce, parental rights, child protection, and family responsibilities.

Overview

This law provides comprehensive regulation of family relationships, protecting the interests of family members and particularly children. It establishes requirements for marriage, grounds for divorce, parental responsibilities, and mechanisms for resolving family disputes.

Key Areas
  • Marriage: Requirements, solemnization, and validation of marriage
  • Divorce: Grounds for divorce and property division procedures
  • Parental Rights: Custody, guardianship, and parental authority
  • Child Protection: Measures for protection of children's interests
  • Alimony: Child support and spousal support obligations
  • Adoption: Procedures for adoption of children

Law on Inheritance

Adopted: 1951 Last Updated: 2023

Establishes rules for succession to property and resolution of inheritance disputes.

Overview

This law governs the transfer of property upon death, whether through a valid will or by the rules of intestate succession. It protects the rights of legitimate heirs and ensures that the deceased's estate is properly distributed according to law.

Key Provisions
  • Testamentary Succession: Requirements for valid wills and testamentary disposition
  • Intestate Succession: Order of inheritance when there is no valid will
  • Forced Heirship: Protection of rights of certain family members
  • Estate Administration: Procedures for inventory and distribution of estate
  • Dispute Resolution: Rules for succession disputes and litigation
