Stay of Removal Orders guidance

A “stay of removal” means delaying or suspending the enforcement of a removal order so you can remain in Canada while pursuing legal options. It is an urgent request often made when removal would cause serious harm or when a legal challenge is underway.

What a Removal Order Means

When an immigration authority issues a removal order, you must leave Canada, and the order becomes enforceable unless it is stayed. Enforcement may include detention, forced departure, and immigration bar consequences.

When a Stay of Removal Is Needed

You may seek a stay of removal if you are:

  • Facing removal but a legal application is pending, such as a judicial review or sponsorship appeal.
  • At risk of serious harm if returned to your home country.
  • In the midst of a complex immigration matter that must be resolved before enforcement.
  • Temporarily unable to depart due to medical, humanitarian or dependent-family circumstances.
