Real Estate Law

Clear, efficient legal support for purchases, sales, refinancing, and property investments across Ontario.

Protecting Your Property Transaction From Start to Finish

Whether you're buying your first home, selling property, or refinancing, real estate transactions involve legal risk.

At BG Laws, we make the process clear and efficient. Our lawyers review every document, protect your interest, and guide you from start to finish. We represent buyers, sellers, and investors in both residential and commercial deals across Ontario.

Residential Real Estate

  • Buying and selling homes or condos.
  • Title searches and registration.
  • Reviewing agreements of purchase and sale.
  • Mortgage and refinancing transactions.
  • Title transfers and survivorship applications.

Commercial Real Estate

  • Buying and selling commercial properties.
  • Reviewing leases and commercial agreements.
  • Structuring corporate ownership and investment.

Investment and Refinance

  • Condo investments and rental property transactions.
  • Private financing and refinancing.
  • Advice on tax and closing costs.
