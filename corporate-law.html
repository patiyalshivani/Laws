Corporate Law

Laws governing business entities, commercial transactions, and corporate governance

Commercial Act

Adopted: 1991 Last Updated: 2024

Regulates commercial transactions, company formation, and business operations in Bulgaria.

Overview

The Commercial Act is the principal legislation governing business entities and commercial transactions in Bulgaria. It establishes the framework for registration of commercial enterprises, defines different types of business structures, and regulates commercial relationships and transactions.

Major Provisions
  • Commercial Enterprises: Types of business entities and registration procedures
  • Sole Traders: Requirements and responsibilities of individual entrepreneurs
  • Partnerships: General partnerships, limited partnerships, and partnership agreements
  • Commercial Companies: Limited liability companies and other corporate structures
  • Company Management: Authority of managers, board of directors, and general meetings
  • Commercial Transactions: Rules for business dealings and contracts
  • Bankruptcy: Procedures for insolvency and business closure
Recent Updates

The Commercial Act has been updated to address e-commerce, digital business models, sustainability requirements, and improved corporate governance standards.

Law on Public Limited Companies

Adopted: 1999 Last Updated: 2023

Governs the establishment, governance, and operation of joint-stock companies in Bulgaria.

Overview

This law establishes the regime for public limited companies (joint-stock companies), providing rules for their formation, capital structure, management, and shareholder rights. It ensures transparency and protection of investor interests.

Key Areas
  • Formation: Requirements for establishing a joint-stock company
  • Share Capital: Issuance, transfer, and trading of shares
  • Shareholders: Rights and responsibilities of shareholders
  • Management: Board of Directors and Executive Management structure
  • Assemblies: General Assemblies of shareholders and procedures
  • Financial Reporting: Accounting and financial disclosure requirements
  • Dividends: Profit distribution to shareholders

Law on Markets in Financial Instruments

Adopted: 2007 Last Updated: 2023

Regulates trading of securities and financial instruments in Bulgaria's financial markets.

Overview

This law establishes the regulatory framework for securities trading, investment services, and financial markets in Bulgaria. It protects investors, ensures market integrity, and establishes rules for brokers and market participants.

Key Provisions
  • Market Regulation: Rules for organized markets and trading venues
  • Investment Firms: Authorization and supervision of brokers and dealers
  • Investor Protection: Rules for customer protection and conflict of interest
  • Trading Rules: Prohibition of market manipulation and insider dealing
  • Disclosure: Requirements for disclosure of material information
  • Supervision: Financial Supervisory Commission authority and enforcement
