Who Can Apply

Spouses or common-law partners of certain foreign nationals in Canada may qualify for an open work permit that allows employment with any eligible employer. This category applies to spouses of skilled workers and spouses of eligible international students.

1. Spouses of Foreign Workers (Principal Foreign National)

A spouse or common-law partner may be eligible for an open work permit if the principal foreign national:

  • Holds a valid work permit, or holds a letter of introduction showing approval for a work permit.
  • Has a work permit valid for at least 16 months at the time the application is submitted.
  • Is working in a qualifying occupation, including TEER 0 or TEER 1 positions, or select TEER 2 and TEER 3 occupations.
  • Continues to remain in Canada with valid status.

Dependent children are generally not eligible for open work permits under this stream.

2. Spouses of International Students

A spouse or common-law partner may also be eligible if the principal foreign national:

  • Holds a valid study permit.
  • Is enrolled in:
    • A master’s program of at least 16 months, or
    • A doctoral program, or
    • A designated professional degree program such as dentistry, medicine, pharmacy, physiotherapy, law, veterinary medicine, or nursing.
  • Maintains full-time student status in an eligible program.

The duration of the spousal open work permit typically matches the length of the principal foreign national's study permit.

How the Application Works

  • The spouse submits an online open work permit application.
  • Proof of relationship is required, such as a marriage certificate or common-law declaration.
  • Proof of the principal foreign national’s valid work permit or study permit, genuine relationship is required.
  • Evidence of the principal foreign national's qualifying occupation or study program is included.
  • The resulting open work permit allows employment for any eligible employer in Canada.
