Labor Law

Laws governing employment relationships, working conditions, and worker rights

Labor Code

Adopted: 1992 Last Updated: 2024

Comprehensive regulation of employment relationships, working conditions, and employee rights in Bulgaria.

Overview

The Labor Code is the primary legislation governing employment law in Bulgaria. It establishes the rights and obligations of employers and employees, regulates working conditions, wages, leave, and termination procedures. The code applies to all employees and employers operating in Bulgaria.

Major Provisions
  • Employment Contracts: Formation, types, and modifications of employment agreements
  • Working Hours: Maximum working hours, overtime, and rest periods
  • Remuneration: Minimum wage, payment terms, and benefits
  • Leave Rights: Paid leave, sick leave, parental leave, and other absences
  • Termination: Procedures for termination of employment and severance
  • Discrimination: Prohibition of discrimination based on protected characteristics
  • Dispute Resolution: Procedures for labor disputes and grievances
Recent Amendments

The Labor Code has been updated to address remote work, flexible work arrangements, parental benefits, and improved protections for vulnerable employees. Recent amendments strengthen protections for workers in platform-based work.

Occupational Safety & Health Law

Adopted: 1997 Last Updated: 2023

Ensures safe and healthy working conditions for all employees by establishing occupational health and safety standards.

Overview

This law establishes requirements for employers to maintain safe working conditions and protect worker health and safety. It includes provisions for hazard assessment, accident reporting, worker training, and compensation for work-related injuries.

Key Components
  • Risk Assessment: Identification and assessment of workplace hazards
  • Safety Measures: Implementation of controls to prevent accidents and illness
  • Equipment: Provision of personal protective equipment and safety gear
  • Training: Worker training on occupational safety
  • Reporting: Requirements for reporting accidents and incidents
  • Inspection: Authority for labor inspectors to investigate workplaces

Law on Collective Labor Disputes and Agreements

Adopted: 1992 Last Updated: 2023

Regulates collective labor relations, union rights, collective agreements, and collective labor disputes.

Overview

This law establishes the framework for collective labor relations in Bulgaria, protecting the right of workers to organize and engage in collective bargaining. It governs the formation of labor unions, negotiation of collective agreements, and procedures for resolving collective disputes.

Key Provisions
  • Union Rights: Right to form and join labor organizations
  • Collective Bargaining: Right to negotiate collective agreements
  • Strike Rights: Right to strike and protection for striking workers
  • Collective Agreements: Validity and enforcement of collective agreements
  • Dispute Resolution: Procedures for mediation and arbitration of labor disputes
