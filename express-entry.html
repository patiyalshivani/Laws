Express Entry guidance

Express Entry is Canada's main system to select skilled workers for permanent residence. It uses a point-based ranking method. Candidates with the strongest profiles receive an invitation to apply for permanent residence.

BG Laws prepares, reviews, and files full Express Entry applications for individuals in Canada and abroad.

Programs Under Express Entry

Canadian Experience Class (CEC)

For skilled workers with recent Canadian work experience.

You must show:

  • At least one year of full-time skilled work in Canada in the last three years.
  • Work experience in TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3.
  • Valid language test results.
  • Status in Canada during the time you worked.

Federal Skilled Worker Program (FSW)

For skilled workers with foreign or Canadian experience.

You must show:

  • One year of continuous skilled work in the last ten years.
  • Work experience in TEER 0, 1, 2 or 3.
  • Minimum language test results.
  • Education assessed by an Educational Credential Assessment.
  • Enough money to settle in Canada.
  • A passing score on the federal selection grid.

Federal Skilled Trades Program (FST)

For individuals with experience in a trade.

You must show:

  • Two years of full-time experience in a trade within the last five years.
  • A certificate of qualification from a Canadian authority or a valid job offer.
  • Minimum language results for each skill.
  • Ability to perform your trade safely in Canada.

This program suits workers in trades such as welding, carpentry, plumbing, electrical work, and related fields.

How Express Entry Works

Step 1. Confirm Eligibility

We confirm which program you fit. We check language tests, education and work history.

Step 2. Create an Express Entry Profile

Candidates enter a pool and receive a Comprehensive Ranking System score. The score depends on age, education, language skills, Canadian experience, foreign experience, job offers and other factors.

Step 3. Receive an Invitation

The Government of Canada conducts draws. Candidates with the strongest scores or those selected in targeted draws receive an invitation to apply.

Step 4. Apply for Permanent Residence

You must submit your full PR application within sixty days. This includes police certificates, medical exams, work letters, funds, and identity documents.

Step 5. Final Decision

If approved, you become a permanent resident of Canada.
