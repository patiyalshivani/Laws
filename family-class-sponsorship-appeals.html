Family Class sponsorship appeal guidance

If you are a Canadian citizen or permanent resident who has sponsored a family member for permanent residence and the visa application was refused, you may be eligible to appeal the refusal through the Immigration Appeal Division (IAD) of the Immigration and Refugee Board of Canada.

Who Can Appeal

You may submit an appeal if:

  • You sponsored a spouse, common-law partner, conjugal partner, dependent child, parent or grandparent under the family class.
  • The visa application for your sponsored family member was refused by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
  • The refusal was not due to inadmissibility for security reasons, organized crime, or serious criminality, in most cases.

When You Cannot Appeal

You cannot appeal if:

  • The sponsored person was found inadmissible on grounds such as security threats, human-rights violations, or serious criminality that preclude sponsorship appeals.
  • The refusal dealt with a category not eligible for appeal under the regulations.

Key Steps in the Appeal Process

  • File a Notice of Appeal to the IAD within the deadline provided in the refusal letter, typically 30 days.
  • After filing, gather and submit your evidence record, including the refusal decision, sponsorship file, supporting documentation and legal arguments.
  • Attend a scheduled hearing, which may include informal resolution or full oral testimony.
  • At the hearing you and your lawyer present your case, the Minister’s counsel responds, and the IAD member decides.
  • The IAD issues a written decision. If the appeal is allowed, the refusal is set aside and the visa application is returned to IRCC for reconsideration. If dismissed, you may explore further legal options such as judicial review.

Why Appeal Matters

An appeal offers a second chance when an initial refusal has serious consequences such as long separation of family members or loss of sponsorship eligibility. A successful appeal restores the sponsorship file to active status and may lead to approval of the visa.
