Parents and Grandparents Program guidance

This program allows Canadian citizens or permanent residents to sponsor their parents or grandparents for permanent residence in Canada. The sponsor commits to providing financial support and abiding by certain requirements so that these family members can settle in Canada.

How the Program Works

  • A Canadian resident submits an “Interest to Sponsor” form when the intake period opens.
  • If selected, the sponsor receives an invitation to apply (ITA).
  • The sponsor then submits a complete sponsorship application and the parent or grandparent submits a permanent residence application.
  • If approved, the sponsored parent or grandparent becomes a permanent resident of Canada.

Sponsor Eligibility

A sponsor must:

  • Be at least 18 years old.
  • Be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or person registered under the Indian Act.
  • Be living in Canada when the application is submitted.
  • Meet or exceed the required income level for the 3 taxation years immediately prior to the application.
  • Sign an undertaking agreeing to support the sponsored parent or grandparent for 20 years (10 years if sponsoring from Quebec).

Who You Can Sponsor

You may sponsor:

  • Parents and grandparents, related by blood or adoption.
  • Dependent children of your parents and grandparents (for example, your siblings, half-siblings, or step-siblings) and their dependent children.
  • If your parents or grandparents are divorced, their current spouse, common-law partner, or conjugal partner.

Income Requirement

Sponsors must show they meet a minimum necessary income (MNI) for each of the 3 tax years before applying. The amount depends on the size of the sponsor’s family unit. If a co-signer is used (spouse or common-law partner), their income may also be considered.
