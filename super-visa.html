Super Visa guidance

The Super Visa allows parents or grandparents of Canadian citizens or permanent residents to stay in Canada for up to five years at a time, with a visa valid for up to ten years.

At BG Laws, we guide you through eligibility, application, and supporting documentation to help bring your loved ones to Canada with confidence.

Eligibility Requirements

For the Applicant (Parent or Grandparent)

  • Be outside Canada when you apply and at the time of approval.
  • Be admissible to Canada, with no serious criminal or immigration issues.
  • Undergo and pass an Immigration Medical Exam, valid for one year.
  • Hold private Canadian medical insurance valid for at least one year from entry, covering healthcare, hospitalisation, and repatriation, with minimum coverage of CAD 100,000.

For the Host (Child or Grandchild in Canada)

  • Must be a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or Registered Indian.
  • Must be at least 18 years old and reside in Canada.
  • Must meet the Minimum Necessary Income (MNI) for the family size.
  • Must provide a Letter of Invitation promising to financially support the visiting parent or grandparent.

Application Considerations

  • Applicants must show temporary intent. Officers assess ties to the home country, finances, and the purpose of the visit to ensure the applicant will leave at the end of the stay.
  • The Super Visa does not lead directly to permanent residence. A separate immigration pathway must be used for that.
  • If a Super Visa is refused, the refusal letter will explain the reasons. Applicants may reapply or request reconsideration if circumstances change.
