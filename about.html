BG Laws is a client-centered law firm serving individuals, families, and businesses across Ontario and beyond. Our Lawyers

HARMANJEET SINGH BAL

Principal Lawyer, Barrister & Solicitor

If you need a dedicated lawyer with courtroom experience and practical advice, Harman Bal offers both.

Harman graduated with an LLB (Hons) and holds a Master's degree in Criminology from Birmingham City University (UK). He practiced as a Solicitor in the United Kingdom for nearly 12 years, representing clients in criminal law, immigration law, and litigation.

After moving to Canada, he completed accreditation through the National Committee on Accreditation (Federation of Law Societies of Canada) and became a member of the Law Society of Ontario, Canadian Bar Association, Ontario Bar Association, and Criminal Lawyers' Association.

Harman brings extensive experience to criminal defence, immigration, and real estate law. He focuses on effective, efficient, and practical solutions tailored to every client's situation. Outside the office, he's a husband, father, and sports enthusiast who values time with family and travel.

Languages: English, Punjabi, Hindi, Urdu.

KANWARPAL SINGH

Associate Lawyer, Barrister & Solicitor

Kanwarpal Singh earned his Bachelor of Laws from Panjab University, Chandigarh (India) in 2019. After moving to Canada, he completed the Paralegal program at Humber College and received accreditation from the National Committee on Accreditation.

He is licensed by the Law Society of Ontario and practices in Immigration, Criminal Defence, and Real Estate Law.

Kanwarpal believes in client-focused advocacy built on preparation, clear communication, and transparency. He represents clients across Ontario with professionalism and respect.

Languages: English, Punjabi, Hindi, Urdu.
