Administrative Law

Laws governing administrative procedures, public administration, and civil service

Administrative Procedure Code

Adopted: 1982 Last Updated: 2023

Regulates procedures for administrative decisions, appeals, and remedies in administrative matters.

Overview

The Administrative Procedure Code establishes the rules governing how government agencies make decisions and how individuals can challenge those decisions. It ensures procedural fairness and protects the rights of citizens in their dealings with the state.

Major Provisions
  • Administrative Acts: Definition, validity, and legal effects of administrative decisions
  • Procedures: Requirements for fair administrative decision-making processes
  • Notice and Hearing: Rights to be heard and present evidence
  • Administrative Appeals: Procedures for appealing decisions to higher authorities
  • Judicial Review: Grounds and procedures for challenging administrative decisions in court
  • Deadlines: Time limits for administrative decisions and filing appeals
  • Legal Remedies: Available remedies for violations of administrative law
Recent Reforms

The Administrative Procedure Code has been reformed to improve transparency, efficiency, and access to digital services. Recent changes emphasize e-government initiatives and faster decision-making timeframes.

Law on Civil Service

Adopted: 1999 Last Updated: 2024

Governs the recruitment, employment, and management of Bulgarian civil servants.

Overview

This law establishes the framework for professional civil service in Bulgaria, setting standards for recruitment, career development, training, and ethical conduct. It ensures that the civil service operates on merit principles and maintains political neutrality.

Key Components
  • Recruitment: Competitive procedures for hiring civil servants
  • Classification: Categorization of civil service positions
  • Rights and Obligations: Work conditions and employee protections
  • Compensation: Salary scales, benefits, and pensions
  • Performance Management: Evaluation and advancement procedures
  • Discipline: Disciplinary procedures and sanctions
  • Professional Development: Training and career advancement opportunities

Law on State Administration

Adopted: 1998 Last Updated: 2023

Establishes the structure and organization of state administrative bodies in Bulgaria.

Overview

This law defines the organization of the executive branch of government, including the Council of Ministers, ministries, and other state administration bodies. It establishes hierarchies, competencies, and procedures for state administration.

Key Provisions
  • Council of Ministers: Composition and functions of the government cabinet
  • Ministries: Organization and responsibilities of government departments
  • Agencies: Establishment and oversight of specialized state agencies
  • Local Administration: Regional and municipal administration structures
  • Coordination: Inter-agency coordination mechanisms
  • Accountability: Accountability and reporting mechanisms
