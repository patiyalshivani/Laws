Canadian travel document guidance

These travel documents are special Canadian travel documents issued to individuals in Canada who are not Canadian citizens but need valid travel papers when they cannot obtain a regular passport from their country of citizenship. These include:

  • Refugee Travel Document: For protected persons or Convention refugees in Canada.
  • Certificate of Identity: For permanent residents or others in Canada who are stateless or cannot obtain a travel document from any other country.

These documents are valid for travel outside Canada but cannot generally be used to travel to your country of citizenship if you still hold citizenship there.

Who Can Apply

You may apply for one of these documents if you are in Canada and meet one of the following:

  • You are recognized as a protected person or a Convention refugee.
  • You are a permanent resident and are stateless or cannot obtain a national passport or travel document from your country.
  • You meet the Canadian immigration status requirements and are physically present in Canada when you apply.
