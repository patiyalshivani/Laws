Criminal Law

Laws governing criminal offenses, punishments, and criminal procedures

Criminal Code

Adopted: 1968 Last Updated: 2024

The comprehensive code that defines criminal offenses, penalties, and principles of criminal liability in Bulgaria.

Overview

The Criminal Code is the fundamental legal instrument that establishes what acts constitute crimes and the corresponding punishments. It applies to all criminal matters within Bulgarian jurisdiction and sets out the principles of criminal responsibility, including issues of intent, negligence, and the circumstances that may eliminate criminal liability.

Major Sections
  • General Provisions: Principles of criminal law and criminal responsibility
  • Crimes Against the Person: Murder, assault, bodily injury crimes
  • Crimes Against Property: Theft, robbery, fraud, embezzlement
  • Crimes Against Public Order: Riots, illegal weapons possession
  • Crimes Against Justice: False testimony, obstruction of justice
  • Penalties: Imprisonment, fines, and other punitive measures
Amendments and Modern Issues

The Criminal Code has been regularly amended to address contemporary issues including cybercrime, terrorism, organized crime, and environmental crimes. Recent amendments have strengthened protections and penalties for crimes against women and children.

Criminal Procedure Code

Adopted: 1974 Last Updated: 2023

Establishes the procedures for investigation, prosecution, and trial of criminal cases in Bulgaria.

Overview

This code regulates all procedural aspects of criminal justice, from the initial investigation through trial and appeals. It ensures the rights of the accused while protecting the public interest and establishing clear procedures for criminal proceedings.

Key Components
  • Investigation Phase: Police and prosecutor investigation procedures
  • Prosecution: Rules for filing charges and pre-trial proceedings
  • Trial Structure: Court procedures, evidence rules, and judicial processes
  • Appeals: Procedures for appealing court decisions
  • Rights of the Accused: Legal rights throughout criminal proceedings
  • Victim Rights: Protections and participation rights for victims

Law on Extradition

Adopted: 1993 Last Updated: 2022

Governs the extradition procedures for individuals wanted for prosecution or punishment in other countries.

Overview

Bulgaria participates in international law enforcement through extradition procedures. This law establishes the conditions under which Bulgaria may extradite individuals to other countries and the protections that apply to ensure fair treatment and individual rights.

Key Provisions
  • Extraditable Offenses: Crimes for which extradition may be granted
  • Exclusions: Categories of persons who cannot be extradited
  • Procedures: Legal procedures for extradition requests
  • Double Criminality: Requirement that conduct be criminal in both countries
  • Human Rights: Protections ensuring compliance with human rights standards
