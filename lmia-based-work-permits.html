LMIA-based work permit guidance

An LMIA-based work permit is issued when a Canadian employer obtains a positive Labour Market Impact Assessment confirming the need to hire a foreign worker. This type of work permit is tied to a specific employer, occupation and location.

To qualify, the employer must show that no Canadian citizen or permanent resident is available to fill the position, and that the job offer meets Canadian wage and labour standards. After the LMIA is approved, the foreign worker applies for a work permit using the LMIA confirmation number and job offer details.

The work permit will specify the employer's name, job title, wage, and employment duration. Any changes in employment normally require a new LMIA and a new work permit application.

Work Permit Length

High-Wage LMIA

For high-wage positions, where the wage offered is at or above the provincial median wage:

  • Employers may request a work permit up to 3 years.
  • This applies to most skilled, supervisory, and professional occupations.

Low-Wage LMIA

For low-wage positions, where the wage is below the provincial median wage:

  • The work permit is usually limited to 1 year.
  • Renewals may be possible, but each renewal requires a new LMIA and is subject to caps and program restrictions.
