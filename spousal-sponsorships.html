Spousal sponsorship guidance

As a Canadian citizen, permanent resident, or registered Indian who is at least 18 years old, you may sponsor your spouse, common-law partner, or conjugal partner for permanent residence in Canada.

The application must be made under one of two classes: the Family Class (out-of-Canada) or the Spouse or Common-Law Partner in Canada Class (in-Canada).

1. Family Class Sponsorship (Out-of-Canada Class)

Use this class when your spouse, common-law partner or conjugal partner lives outside Canada, or lives in Canada but does not intend to stay in Canada while the application is processed.

Key features

  • Your partner applies from abroad, or from Canada but will leave during processing.
  • After approval, your partner enters Canada as a permanent resident.
  • You and your partner can generally travel in and out of Canada while the application is pending.
  • There is a right of appeal if the application is refused.
  • The sponsor must sign an undertaking promising to provide financial support for the basic needs (food, clothing, shelter, health) of the sponsored partner and any dependent children for the required period.
  • The typical undertaking length for a spouse or partner is 3 years from the date the partner becomes a permanent resident.

2. Spouse or Common-Law Partner in Canada Class (In-Canada Class)

Use this class when your spouse or common-law partner lives with you in Canada and has valid temporary status (visitor, worker or student), or is exempt from needing such status.

Key features

  • Both sponsor and partner are physically in Canada at the time of application.
  • The partner may be eligible to apply for an open work permit while the permanent residence application is being processed.
  • Typically there is no appeal right; a refusal means a new application or judicial review may be needed.
  • Travel outside Canada during processing may carry risks of being unable to return.
  • The same undertaking requirement applies: you must support your partner financially for the required period.

Eligibility & Obligations for Both Classes

  • The sponsor must be 18 years or older, a Canadian citizen or permanent resident (or registered Indian), and living in Canada or plan to live in Canada when the partner becomes a permanent resident.
  • The sponsor must demonstrate ability to meet the basic needs of themselves, the sponsored partner, and any dependent children.
  • The sponsored partner must pass medical, criminal, security and admissibility checks.
  • Proof of a genuine relationship is required, including a marriage certificate, common-law cohabitation for at least 12 months, or conjugal partner evidence if applicable.
  • For spouses or partners living with you in Canada under the in-Canada class, your relationship must be properly documented and co-residence shown.
  • You cannot sponsor if you are in default of previous sponsorship obligations, have unpaid social assistance or court-ordered support payments, or are under certain removal orders.
