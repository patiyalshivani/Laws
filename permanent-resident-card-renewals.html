Permanent Resident Card renewal guidance

A Permanent Resident (PR) card is the main proof of your legal status in Canada. It is required for air travel and is commonly needed for employment, identity verification and government services. While your PR status does not expire, your PR card does, and renewal is essential to avoid travel problems or delays accessing services.

BG Laws assists permanent residents with smooth, accurate and timely PR card renewal applications.

Why PR Card Renewal is Important

  • A PR card is usually valid for five years.
  • You must show a valid PR card to board a flight back to Canada.
  • If your card expires while you are abroad, you may need a Permanent Resident Travel Document to return.
  • A valid PR card makes it easier to prove your identity to employers, banks and government agencies.

Renewing early prevents unexpected travel issues and proof-of-identity delays.

Eligibility for PR Card Renewal

To renew your PR card, you must:

  • Be a permanent resident of Canada.
  • Be physically present in Canada when you submit your application.
  • Not be subject to a removal order.
  • Not have voluntarily given up your PR status.
  • Have a Canadian address for delivery.

You must also meet the residency obligation, unless you qualify under an exemption.

Residency Obligation: The 730-Day Rule

To maintain PR status, you generally must have spent at least 730 days (two years) in Canada during the last five-year period. These 730 days do not need to be consecutive.

You may still meet the obligation even if you were outside Canada if:

  • You accompanied a Canadian-citizen spouse or parent.
  • You were employed full-time by a Canadian business while outside Canada.
  • You accompanied a permanent resident spouse or parent who was working for a Canadian business.
  • You can show humanitarian or compassionate reasons for not meeting the requirement.

BG Laws assesses your full travel history to ensure compliance before filing.

When You Should Apply

Apply when:

  • Your card will expire within nine months.
  • Your card has already expired.
  • You need your card for upcoming travel or employment.
  • You have recently returned to Canada after long periods abroad.

It is better to renew early rather than risk travel difficulties.
