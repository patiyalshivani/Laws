Visitor Visa guidance

Canada's visitor visa system is designed for travel, tourism, or visiting family and friends. The Temporary Resident Visa (TRV) permits entry to Canada for a temporary stay, typically up to six months.

Our team at BG Laws supports you through each step of the visitor visa process, whether you're applying from abroad or exploring opportunities to extend your stay.

Key Requirements & What You'll Need

To obtain a Visitor Visa (TRV), you must satisfy the immigration officer that you:

  • Hold a valid travel document, such as a passport.
  • Are admissible to Canada, with no serious criminal or immigration issues.
  • Will leave Canada at the end of your stay.
  • Have sufficient funds to support your stay and return home.
  • Do not intend to work or study in Canada unless already authorised.

Typical Supporting Documents Include

  • Valid passport.
  • Completed and signed application forms.
  • Proof of financial ability, such as bank statements and income letters.
  • Travel itinerary or round-trip ticket and hotel bookings.
  • Letter of invitation if visiting family or friends.
  • Proof of ties to your home country, including employment, property, and family commitments.
  • Letter of intent outlining the purpose of the visit and your plan to depart.
  • Biometric information, including fingerprints and a photo, if required.
  • Medical examination if required by your country of residence.
